# The board's sounds

All from [Lichess](https://lichess.org)
([lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/sound)), converted from
MP3 to 16-bit mono WAV (`afconvert`), otherwise unchanged. Each set has a move, a capture and a
check.

- `sfx`, `piano`, `nes`, `futuristic`: by [Enigmahack](https://github.com/Enigmahack), under the
  GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later (AGPLv3+). Kiebitz is GPLv3, whose
  section 13 allows combining it with AGPLv3 works; these files stay under the AGPLv3+.
- `standard`, `woodland`: Lichess's own sets. Lichess's COPYING.md lists them among its
  exceptions, not under a free licence. They are included here as other open-source chess apps
  (En Croissant) do; if Lichess or their author asks, they will be taken out or replaced.
